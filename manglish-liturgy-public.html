Karthaavinte Prarthana (Matthai 6:9-13)
P:
Swarggasthanaaya njangalude Pithaave
J:
Ninte naamam vishudheekarikkappadename; ninte rajyam varename; ninte ishtam swargathileppole - bhoomiyilum aakename; njangalkku aavashyamulla aaharam innu tharename; njangalude kadakkarodu njangal kshamichirikkunnathupole - njangalude kadangale njangalodum kshamikkename; njangale pareekshayil kadathathe - dushtangal ninnu njangale viduvikkename; rajyamum shakthiyum mahathvavum - ennekkum ninakkullathallo. Apeksha
P:
Dhaivamaya Karthaave, bhagyam nalkunnavayum - prakasham niranjirikkunnavayumaya - ninte rashmigalaal njangalude aathmeeya kannukale prakashippikkename. Marichavarude idayil ninnulla ninte uyirthezhunnelppinte ee divasathil njangale santhoshippikkename. Ninte karthruthvathinte udhayathal njangale aanandhippikkename. Njangalude jeevante prathyaashayum, njangalude aathmaakkalude rakshithavum aaya njangalude Karthaavum Dhaivavumaya Mashihaye, ninte kripayude sahayangal kondu njangale thunakkukayum cheyyename.

Aamen.

Sthothra Geetham
P:
Athyunnathangalil swargeeya malaaghamar sthuthikkunnathupole balaheenarum kuttakkaarumaaya njangalum sthuthikkunnu.
J:
Ellaa kaalavum ellaa samayavum unnathangalil Dhaivathinu sthuthiyum - bhoomiyil samaadhaanavum nirappum - manushyarkku nalla prathyashayum undaayirikkatte. Sarvashakthiyulla Pithaavayi - swargeeya Rajaavayi- Srishtavaaya Karthaave- ninne njangal sthuthikkukayum vazhthukayum vandikkukayum - sthuthiyude shabdham ninakku njangal karettukayum - ninte mahathvathin shreshtatha nimitham -ninakku njangal sthothram cheyyukayum cheyyunnu. Vishudhatmavinodukoode Yeshumashiha aaya eka Puthranaam Dhaivame,- Pithaavinte Puthranum vachanavum - lokathinte papathe chumannozhikkunna -Dhaivakunjadum aaya Karthaave - njangalodu karuna cheyyename. Lokathinte paapathe neekikalayunnavane, - ninte chevi chaayichu njangalude apeksha kaikkolllename- ninte Pithaavinte valathubhagathu mahathvathodirukkunnavane - dhayathonni njangale anugrahikkename. Enthanennal nee maathram parishudhanaakunnu. Pithaavaya Dhaivathinte mahathvathinu Vishudhatmavinodukoode Yeshumashiha aaya nee mathram Karthaavaakunnu. Aamen.
Parishudhathma Niravinuvendiyulla Apeksha
P:
Swargasthnaya njangalude Pithaave, ninte Puthrane ayachu ninne thanneyum ninte pithrusnehatheyum - aa sneham nalkunna sakala anugrahangaleyum - njangalkku velippeduthiyathinal ninne njangal sthuthikkunnu. Penthakosthu naalil ayachuthanna Parishudhaathmaavinal njangale niraykkanamennu njangal vanchichu prarthikkunnu. Paranju theeratha aathmavinte anugrahangalum Dhaivasnehavum njangalude hridhayangalilekku choriyename. Kristhuvinte manassum jeevanum kondu niranju Yesuvineppole aakuvanum Pithaavinte snehathil vasikkuvanum njangale praptharakkename. Pithaave, njangalude jeevitha vijayathinum pravarthanangalude unnamathinum Parishudhaathmaavinal njangale shakthippuduthename. Chodikkunnavarkkum, vishvasikkunnavarkkum, anusarikkunnavarkkum Parishudhaathmaavine nalkamennulla vagdathathil vishvasichukondum - aviduthe kalpanakale anusarichukondum - aduthuvannu yachikkunna aviduthe makkale Parishudhaathmaavukondu niraykkename. Aathmavinte phalam purappeduvechu thirumeniye mahathvappeduvanum, divya varangalaal Dhaiveeka vyapaaram cheythu yajamananau aadhaaayam varuthuvanum - njangale sahayikkukayum - ninte sabhaye shakthipputhukayum cheyyumaragkename.
J:

Karthave, ivar praveshikkunna ee puthuvatsarathil ninte puthiya kripakal ivarkku daanam cheyyename. Ninte samruddhiyaaya jeevan kondu ivare niraykkename. Thiruvachana dhyaanathilum praarthanayilum poorvaadhikam thaalparyavum Dhaiveekaaraadhanayilum thiruvathazhattilum sambandhikkunnathinu koothuthalaaya aakaamkshayum ivarkku nalkename. Vivaha varshika sthothra praarthana
(Vivaha varshikamullavar praarthanakkaayi munpil vannu nilkkanam)
P:
Skala nanmakaludeyum anugrahangaludeyum daathaavaaya Dhaivame, njangal vyakthikalayum kudumbangalayum ninkal ninnupraapichirikkunna ellavidha nanmakalkkum, kripakalkkumayi angaye vaazhthi sthuthikkunnu. Swargaththilum bhoomiyilumulla sakala kudumbathinum peru varuvaan kaaranamaaya Dhaivathe njangal vaazhthippaadunnu. Bhoomiyil kudumbangale sthapikkunnavanum avaye anugrahikkunnavanumaaya snehanidhivaaya Dhaivame, njangaleude kudumbangalkkaayum kudumba bandhangalkkaayum njangal angaye vaazhthukayum mahatvappeduthukayum cheyyunnu.

Ippol vivaha varshika sthothra praarthanakkaayi thirusannidhiyil aduthuvanneirikkunna ee bhaaryabharthanmarkkaayi ninne vaazhthunnu. Kazhinja oru varsham ivarkku vyakthikalayum kudumbangalayum nalkiya ella nanmakalkku vendiyum njangal sthuthi sthothrangal arppikkunnu. Priya Pithaave santhoshaththilum santhapaththilum, sampaththilum daaridryaththilum aarogyaththilum roagaththilum thammil snehikkukayum, shushrooshikkukayum, santhushtippaduthuvanum ivar sabhayudeyum, sakala hridayangale shodhana cheyyunna Dhaivathinte sannidhiyilum vivahanalil cheythittulla theerumanam sadaa ormmichu anthyaththolam athinprakaaram jeevichu bhoomiyil orumichu ninne mahimappeduthuvaan kripa arulenam. Ivar kudumbapraarthanayilum thiruvachana dhyaanathilum, aathmeeya shushrooshakalilum, aavashyathilirikkunnavare sahayikkunnathilum, roagikale shushrooshikkunnathilum utsukarayi irikkunnathinum, "njanoom ente kudumbavumo, njangal yahovaye sevikkum" ennu yoshuvayepole ettu paranjum maathrika kudumbamaayi jeevikkunnathinum kripa arulename. Ivar praveshikkunna puthuvasham anugrahathinteyum nanmakaludeyum varshamaakkename. Ivarude makkaleyum, maathapithakaleyum, sahodharangaleum bandhumithraadhikaleum anugrahichu aashirvadikkenam. Dhaivame, innivide vannucheeruvaan saadhikkathe poyittullavareyum njangal thirusannidhiyil orkkukayum avarkkuvendiyum praarthikkukayum cheyyunnu. Avarkkum aavashyamulla ella kripakalum anugrahangalum nalki ninte sarvashakthiyulla bhujangalin keezhe avareyum marachukollename. Njangaleude ella kudumbangalum ninne mahatvappeduthunna kudumbangalayirippaanum, thandhinmel prakashikkunna vilakkupoleyum, malamel shobhikkunna pattanaam poleyum ayirippaanum evarayum anugrahikkename. Njangal Ninakkum, ninte Puthranum, Parishuddhaathmaavinum sthuthiyum sthothravum karettikkollunnu.
