Karthavinte Prarthana (Matthai 6:9-13)
P:
Swargasthnaya njangalude Pithave
J:
Ninte namam vishudhikarikkappadename; ninte rajyam varename; ninte ishtam bhoomiyilum swargathileppole aakename; njangalkku aavashyamulla aaharam innu tharename; njangalude kadakkarodu njangal kshamichirikkunnathupole njangalude kadangale njangalodum kshamikkename; njangale pareekshayil kadathathe dushtangal ninnu njangale viduvikkename; rajyamum shakthiyum mahathvavum ennekkum ninakkullathallo. Aamen.
Apeksha
P:
Daivamaya Karthave, bhagyam nalkunnavayum prakasham niranjirikkunnavayumaya ninte rashmigalala njangalude aathmiya kannukale prakashippikkename. Marichavarude idayil ninnulla ninte uyarthezhunnelppinte ee divasathil njangale santhoshippikkename. Ninte karthruthvathinte udayathal njangale aanandhippikkename. Njangalude jeevante prathyashayum, njangalude aathmakkalude rakshithavum aaya njangalude Karthavum Daivamaya Mashihaye, ninte krupayude sahayangal kondu njangale thunakkukayum cheyyename.
J:
Aamen.

Njangalude papangale mochichirikkunnu ennulla dridhavishvasathil njangal ninakku sthuthiyum sthothravum karettuvan Mashiha nimitham njangale arharakkitheerkukayum cheyyename.

Aamen.

Sthothra Geetham
P:
Athyunnathangalil swargeeya malaghamar sthuthikkunnathupole balahinarum kuttakkarumaya njangalum sthuthikkunnu.
J:
Ellakalavum ella samayavum unnathangalil Daivathinu sthuthiyum - bhoomiyil samadhanavum nirappum - manushyarkku nalla prathyashayum undayirikkatte. Sarvashakthiyulla Pithavayi swargeeya Rajavayi- Srishtavaya Karthave- ninne njangal sthuthikkukayum vazhthukayum vandikkukayum sthuthiyude shabdham ninakku njangal karettukayum - ninte mahathvathin shreshtathanimitham -ninakku njangal sthothram cheyyukayum cheyyunnu. Vishudhatmavinodukoode Yeshumashiha aaya eka Puthranam Daivame,- Pithavinte Puthranum vachanavum lokathinte papathe chumannozhikkunna Daivakunjadum aaya Karthave - njangalodu karuna cheyyename. Lokathinte papathe neekikalayunnavane, - ninte chevi chayichu njangalude apeksha kaikkolllename- ninte Pithavinte valathubhagathu mahathvathodirukkunnavane dayathonni njangale anugrahikkename. Enthanennal nee mathram parishudhanakunnu. Pithavaya Daivathinte mahathvathinu Vishudhatmavinodukoode Yeshumashiha aaya nee mathram Karthavakunnu. Aamen.
Parishudhatma Niravinuvendiyulla Apeksha
P:
Swargasthnaya njangalude Pithave, ninte Puthrane ayachu ninnetthanneyum ninte pithrusnehatheyum aa sneham nalkunna sakala anugrahangaleyum njangalkku velippeduthiyathinal ninne njangal sthuthikkunnu. Penthakosthunalal ayachuthanna Parishudhatmavinal njangale niraykkanamennu njangal vanchichu prarthikkunnu. Paranju theeratha aathmavinte anugrahangalum daivasnehavum njangalude hridayangalilekku choriyename. Kristhuvinte manassum jeevanum kondu niranju Yesuvineppole aakuvanum Pithavinte snehathil vasikkuvanum njangale praptharakkename. Pithave, njangalude jeevitha vijayathinum pravarthanangalude unnamathinum Parishudhatmavinal njangale shakthipputhename. Chodikkunnavarkkum, vishvasikkunnavarkkum, anusarikkunnavarkkum Parishudhatmavine nalkamennulla vagdathathil vishvasichukondum aviduthe kalpanagale anusarichukondum aduthuvennu yachikkunna aviduthe makkale Parishudhatmavukondu niraykkename. Aathmavinte phalam purappeduvechu thirumeniye mahathvappeduvanum, divya varangalal daiveeka vyaparam cheythu yajamananau aadhayam varuthuvanum njangale sahayikkukayum ninte sabhaye shakthipputhukayum cheyyumaragkename.
J:
Aamen.
(Allenkil)
P:
Parishudhatmavuvennu papathevroichum, neethiyechroichum, nyayavidhiyechroichum lokathinu bodham varuthum ennu kalpicharulicheytha Karthave, ee Parishudhatmavinte adhivasam njangalil eppozhum undayirikkename. Papathinte bhayankara phalangal - athinte mechatha, athilulla nandikedu ivayeppatti yatharthamaya bodham njangalkku tharename. Neethibodhamillatha njangalude pravruthigalum vyakthiparavum samajikavumaya njangalude aneethigalum moolam njangaludemelum njangalude vargathinmelum varuthivaykkunna bhayankara nyayavidhiyechkurichulla bodham njangalil vardhippikkename. Ninte Parishudhatmavinal njangale nirachu - thettukalil ninnu njangale akatti njangalude kashtathakalil njangale aashvasippichu njangale ninte ishtaprakaram niyanthrichu bharikkename. Ninte aathmavu njangale ella sathyathilum neethiyilum vazhinadathi ee lokathil ninte prasadam anubhavikkunna jeevithavum swargalokathil ninte makkalkkayi orukkiyirikkunna mokshanandavum njangalkku anubhavamakukayum cheyyename.

Aamen.
