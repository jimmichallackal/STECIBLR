A Cheerful Giver

Giving is an act of worship and gratitude. We encourage everyone to support the mission and ministry of the church and parish willingly, out of a heart filled with love and thankfulness for God's endless blessings.

"Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver."
— 2 Corinthians 9:7
