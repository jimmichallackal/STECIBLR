Prayer of Repentance (1)
Min:
Almighty and eternal God our Father, You are light to those in darkness, strength to the weak, and the only refuge to those who fall into sin. We praise You for this occasion to confess and forsake our manifold wretched sins. We call upon Your mercy in confidence of Your acceptance of all those who come to You with a contrite heart. Help us to offer our praise and thanksgiving to You in the assurance that our sins are forgiven. Hear us, we beseech You, in the name of our Lord, the Messiah.
Con:
Amen
Prayer of Repentance (2)
Min:
Almighty and most merciful Father, we have erred and strayed from Your ways like lost sheep. We have followed the devices and desires of our own hearts. We have offended against Your holy laws. We have left undone those things which we ought to have done; and there is no health in us. But You O Lord, have mercy upon us miserable offenders. Spare them, O God, who confess their faults. Restore them that are penitent, according to Your promises declared unto mankind in Christ Jesus our Lord. And grant us O merciful Father, for His sake that we may hereafter live a godly, righteous and sober life, to the glory of Your Holy name.
Con:
Amen
Prayer of Repentance (3)
Min:
O God, Who is merciful to sinners, have mercy upon us in your judgement. Forgive us our sins according to the greatness of Your loving kindness. We who have been afflicted, knock at Your door O most merciful. Grant us our supplications by Your grace. O Merciful Lord, our Messiah, open to us the gate of Your blessing, for You receive the offenders who would turn to You and call upon you in repentance. Heavenly Father, we pray that You hear our petition and bless us. Praise be to you O Lord who has said, "Call upon Me and I will answer; knock and I will open; ask and I will give.
Con:
O Lord, the owner of all those in heaven, and the refuge of all those here on earth, hear our prayer and bestow upon us Your blessing.

Prayer for the Holy Spirit (1)
Min:
Our heavenly father, who has sent your Son and revealed to us yourself, your fatherly love, and all the blessings of your love. You have also taught us that you would give the Holy Spirit in abundance to them that ask You. O Father, we pray earnestly for the fullness of the Holy Spirit. We do make supplications for the untold blessings of the spirit. It is your Holy Spirit who shed abroad your love into our hearts. It is the Holy Spirit who enables us to be filled with the mind and life of Christ, and thus to become like Jesus and to abide in the love of the father. It is the Holy Spirit who strengthens us from above for our service and activities. We come near unto You O God, trusting in Your precious promises, that the Holy Spirit will be given to them that ask, and to them that obey, and we beseech you O Father, believing in Your faithfulness and obeying Your commandments, that we Your humble children may be filled with The Holy Spirit. Help us to glorify you with the fruit of the Spirit such as love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith, meekness, and temperance (Gal. 5:22-23). Also help us to bring much gain unto our Master by making use of the spiritual gifts which you freely bestow upon us. We pray to you O lord that the holy spirit, who was sent upon your church on the day of Pentecost. may be given in His fullness unto us and to all the believers and that your church may thus be empowered.
Con:
Amen
Prayer for the Holy Spirit (2)
Min:
O lord, who has said that when the Holy Spirit comes He will convict the world of sin, righteousness and judgement, may this Spirit dwell in us always. Grant us a sense of the terrible consequences of sin and its abomination, and the ungratefulness involved in it. Give us an increasing sense of the terrible consequences of our unjust and sinful actions. on us individuals and groups and society. We pray that your. Holy Spirit may dwell in us and keep us away from evil, and direct and control us according to Your will. Let your Spirit guide us in all truth and righteousness so that we may lead a life in this world well pleasing b You and enter into the joy of the salvation prepared for Your children in the world to come.

Amen
