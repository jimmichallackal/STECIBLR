Maranam Dhaivavachanathinte Velichathil

"Avanavan shareerathil irikkumbol cheythathu nallathaakilum theeyathaakilum athinu thakkavannam praapikkendathinu naam ellaavarum Khristuvinte nyaayaasanathinte mumpaake velippedendathaakunnu." 2 Korinthyar 5:10

"Athrayumalla njangalkkum ningalkkum naduve valiyoru pilarppundaakkiyirikkunnu. Ividennu ningalude adukkal kadannuvaruvaan ichikkunnavarkku kazhivilla; avidennu njangalude adukkal kadannuvaruvaanum paadilla ennu paranju."
Lukkos 16:26

"Njaan swargathilninnu oru shabdham kettu; athu paranjathu: ezhuthuka: innumuthal karthaavil marikkunna mrithanmaar bhagyavaanmaar; athe, avar thangalude prayathnangalilninnu vishramikkendathaakunnu; avarude pravrithi avare pinthudarunnu ennu aathmaavu parayunnu." Velippaadu 14:13

"Ithu paranjittu avan: nammude snehithanaaya Laasar nidrakollunnu; enkilum njaan avane unarthuvaan pokunnu ennu avarodu paranju. "Yohannaan 11:11

"Ennal anthya kaahalanaadhathinkal pettennu kannimaykkunnidatil naam ellaavarum roopantharappedum. Kaahalam dhvanikkum; marichavar akshayaraayi uyirkkukayum naam roopantharappedukayum cheyyum." 1 Korinthyar 15:52

"Thante bhakthanmaarude maranam Yehovaykku vilayeriyathaakunnu." Sankeerthanangal 116:15

“Enikku jeevikkunnathu Khristuvum marikkunnathu laabhavumaakunnu.” Philippiyar 1:21.

“Koodaaramaaya njangalude bhavanam azhinjupoyaal kaippaniyallaatha nithyabhavanamaayi Dhaivathinte daanamaayoru kettidam njangalkku swargathil undennu ariyunnu” 2 Korinthyar 5:1.

"Makkal jadarakthangalodu koodiyavar aakakondu avanum avareppole jadarakthangalodu koodiyavanaayi maranathinte adhikaariyaaya pishaachine thante maranatthaal neekki jeevaparyantham maranabheethiyaal adimakalaayirunnnavareyokkeyum viduvichu." Ebraayar 2:14,15.

“Aadaamil ellaavarum marikkunnathupole Khristuvil ellaavarum jeevikkappedum. Orooruthanum thaanthaante nirayilathre; aadyaphalam Khristu; pinne Khristuvinullavar avante varavinkal”
1 Korinthyar 15:22,23.

“Nanma cheythavar jeevanaayum thinma cheythavar nyaayavidhikkaayum punaruthaanam cheyyuvaanulla naazhika varunnu.” Yohannaan 5:29.

“Yeshu marikkukayum jeevichu ezhunnelkkukayum cheythu ennu naam vishvasikkunnu enkil angane thanne Dhaivam nidrakkondavareyum Yeshu mukhaantharam avanodukoode varuthum. Karthaavinte prathyakshathavare jeevanode sheshikkunnvaraaya naam nidrakkondavarkku mumpaakayilla ennu njangal karthaavinte vachanatthaal ningalodu parayunnu.”1 Thessalonikyar 4:14-15.

"Karthaavaakunnu thaan gambheeranaadhathodum pradhaana doothante shabdhatthodum Dhaivathinte kaahalatthodum koode swargathilninnu irangi varikayum Khristuvil marichavar mumpe uyirthezhunnelkkukayum cheyyum, pinne jeevanode sheshikkunna naam avarodum orumichu aakashathil karthaavine ethirelppaan meghangalil edukkappedum. Ingane naam eppozhum karthaavinodu koode irikkum” 1 Thessalonikyar 4:16-17.

Praarambha Prarthana
P:
“Njaan thanne punaruthaanavum jeevanum aakunnu; ennil vishvasikkunnavan marichaalum jeevikkum; jeevichirunnu ennil vishvasikkunnavan aarum orunaalum marikkilla” ennu njangalkku urappu nalkiyirunna karthaave, njangal ninakku sthothram cheyyunnu. Jeevakaramaya avidathe vaagdathangalorthu thirunaamathe njangal vazhthunnu. Maranathinupolum njangale ninte snehathilninnu akattuvaan saadhyamallaaykaayaal ninakku sthuthi.

Njangalude karthaave, njangal balaheenarum, vishvasathil kuravullavarumaakayaal, agnishodhanayil thottupokaathe ninte balamulla kaikalal njangale thaangikollename. Karthaave, njangalude sankadangaludeyum niraashakaludeyum sandharbhangale prathyaashayudeyum dhairyathinteyum avasarangalaayi nee roopantharappeduthename.

Sneham niranjja Pithaave, bhaarappettum dhukhichumirikkunna ee ninte makkaleyum sakala buddhiyeyum kaviyunna dhaiveeka samaadhaanatthaal samaashvasippikkename. Ee lokathile njangalude bandhangal thaalkaalikamaakunnuvallo; njangalkku ivide sthiramaya vaasavum vaasasthalamum illallo. Ennal njangal Dhaivathinte makkalaakayaal swarggeeya paurathvathinte prathyaasha njangalkkundu. Ee lokam Dhaivajanakalkku yogyamallaallo. Nee lokatheyum maranatheyum jayichavanaakayaal njangalkkum aa jayathil nee avakaasham nalkiyirikkunnu. Ee santhoshakaramaya arivodukoodi pakalullappol ayachavante vela thikachukondu, prathyaashayodukoodi jeevikkuvaan njangale sahaayikkename. “Karthaavu thannu, karthaaveduthu, karthaavinte naamam vazhthappedu maarakatte.” Ennu paranju dhaivahithathinu keezhppettirikkaan njangale sahaayikkename. Maranatheyum paathaalatheyum tholppichu njangalkku uyirpinteyum nithyajeevanteyum prathyaasha daanam cheythirikkunna priya rakshakante naamathil njangalude prarthana kelkkumaaraakename.

Dhaivathinte Ekaputhranum...
J:
Sarvalokangalkkum mumpe Pithaavilninnu janichavanum - prakaashathil ninnulla prakaashavum - sathya Dhaivathil ninnulla sathya Dhaivavum - janichavanum, srishtiyallaathavanum, thathvathil Pithaavinodu ekathvamullavanum - sakala srishtikkum mukhaantharamaayavanum - manushyaraaya njangalkkum njangalude rakshaykkum vendi - swargathil ninnirangi, Parishudhaathmaavinaal Kanyakamariyaamil ninnu - shareeram dharichu manushyanaayi - Ponthiyos Peelathosinte naalukalil, njangalkkuvendi krooshikkappattu - kashtathayanubhavichu marichu adakkappattu - thiruhithaprakaaram moonnaam divasam uyirthezhunnettu - swargathilekku kareri - thante Pithaavinte valathubhaagathirikkunnavanum - avasaanamillaatha raajathvamullavanum - jeevichirikkunnavareyum marichavareyum vidhippaan, thante mahaprabhaavatthode iniyum varuvaanirikkunnavanumaaya, Yeshu Mashiha aaya eka Karthaavilum njangal vishvasikkunnu.
